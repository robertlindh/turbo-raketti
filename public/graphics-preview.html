TurboRaketti — pixel-art direction comparison

Three takes on pixel-art for the remake. Each panel shows a ship, cave wall + floor, a bullet, and a thrust flame at the same in-game scene. Pick the one that feels right and I'll port the style into the live game.

01Amiga-faithful 16-color

128×96 logical 16 colours no AA

Hard pixels, saturated palette, solid-fill shading, dithered transitions. Speaks the same visual language as the 1992 original — but with modern composition, lighting, and animation.

  • Recognisable as a tribute to the original
  • Cheapest to produce — small sprites, few colours
  • Will look chunky on 4K monitors (which is the charm)

02Modern indie pixel

128×96 logical ~32 colours subtle dither

Same pixel resolution but a richer palette with smooth in-grid shading and dithering. The look of Celeste, Hotline Miami, Hyper Light Drifter. Most flexibility — readable at any zoom, gameplay-first, still feels handmade.

  • Best balance of style and clarity
  • Industry-standard for 2D indie in 2026
  • Easiest to extend with sprite-sheet animation

03HD pixel art + glow

192×144 logical unlimited colours bloom

Higher resolution sprites with full shading, soft lighting and additive glow on engines and bullets — Dead Cells / Sea of Stars territory. Pixel art aesthetic but cinematic. Most production effort per asset.

  • Most striking screenshots
  • Lighting and particles do a lot of work
  • Requires more time per sprite — slowest to iterate
